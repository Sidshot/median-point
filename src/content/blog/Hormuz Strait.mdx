As of **April 5, 2026**, the international order is experiencing a systemic shock. The military confrontation between the Islamic Republic of Iran and the combined military forces of the United States and Israel has reached a critical juncture, defined by an effective blockade of the **Strait of Hormuz** and a chilling 48-hour ultimatum from Washington that threatens to expand the theater of war into a total regional conflagration. ## I. The "Original Sin": 1953 and the Architecture of Resentment

The modern conflict finds its primary causal roots in the events of August 1953, a period that serves as the "original sin" in the narrative of Iranian sovereignty. The democratically elected Prime Minister Mohammad Mosaddegh was overthrown in a coup d'état orchestrated by the CIA and British intelligence. The motivation was primarily economic: Mosaddegh had moved to nationalize the Anglo-Iranian Oil Company. This intervention restored Mohammad Reza Shah Pahlavi to absolute power, initiating a 25-year period of autocratic rule characterized by intense Western alignment and internal suppression.

The Shah's tenure, while marked by economic modernization known as the White Revolution, created deep cultural and religious fissures. The United States, viewing Iran as a bulwark against Soviet expansion, provided largely unfettered support, including the 1957 "Atoms for Peace" agreement, which ironically laid the foundation for the very nuclear program that is the focal point of today's strikes.

| Year | Event              | Geopolitical Consequence                                        |
| ---- | ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1953 | Operation Ajax     | Removal of Mosaddegh; restoration of the Pahlavi monarchy.      |
| 1957 | Atoms for Peace    | Establishment of Iran's civil nuclear infrastructure by the US. |
| 1979 | Islamic Revolution | Collapse of the monarchy; rise of anti-Western governance.      |

The 1979 Revolution fundamentally inverted the regional status quo, transforming Iran into a vehemently anti-Western theocracy. The subsequent 444-day Iran Hostage Crisis solidified this adversarial relationship, leading to a permanent severance of diplomatic ties on April 7, 1980.

## II. The Shadow War and the Proxy Architecture

While the United States sought containment, Israel viewed the post-1979 Iranian regime as an existential threat. The conflict transitioned into a sophisticated "shadow war" of cyberwarfare, maritime sabotage, and targeted assassinations. Iran's creation of the "**Axis of Resistance**"—a network including Hezbollah, Hamas, and various militias in Iraq and Syria—was designed to encircle Israel and provide Tehran with strategic depth.

Israel's response focused on the "Campaign Between Wars," aimed at delaying Iran's nuclear program and degrading its missile capabilities. A landmark operation was the 2010 deployment of the **Stuxnet** worm, which destroyed over 1,000 Iranian centrifuges.

* **April 13, 2024**: Iran retaliated with over 300 drones and missiles directed at Israeli territory, setting the precedent for the direct state-on-state violence of 2025-2026.

## III. The Twelve-Day War of June 2025

The current conflict traces specifically to the collapse of the 2025 Muscat-Rome negotiations. Following the return of President Trump to the White House, a "maximum pressure" campaign was reinstated. The discovery of record amounts of military-grade enriched uranium on May 31, 2025, terminated the diplomatic track.

On **June 13, 2025**, Israel launched "**Operation Rising Lion**," a surprise airstrike campaign targeting facilities in Tehran, Fordow, and Natanz. This triggered the "Twelve-Day War"

* **June 19, 2025**: Iran struck Beersheba, damaging the Soroka Medical Center.
* **June 22, 2025**: The U.S. joined the war with "**Operation Midnight Hammer**," utilizing B-2 stealth bombers and bunker-buster bombs at Fordow and Isfahan.
* **June 24, 2025**: A total ceasefire took effect.

The economic impact caused the Iranian rial to collapse to 1.45 million per US dollar by late 2025. This sparked massive protests, including the "Tehran's Tank Man" incident on December 29, 2025. The regime's crackdown resulted in an estimated 6,800 civilian deaths.

## IV. 2026: Operation Epic Fury and Decapitation

On **February 28, 2026**, the U.S. and Israel launched "**Operation Epic Fury**," designed for regime paralysis and collapse. The operation began with nearly 900 joint strikes in the first 12 hours. The most significant event was the **assassination of Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei** mid-morning on February 28.

The Assembly of Experts subsequently appointed his son, **Mojtaba Khamenei**, as the new Supreme Leader, representing a "doubling down" by the IRGC. This new leadership took an uncompromising stance, effectively closing the Strait of Hormuz on March 4, 2026.

## VII. Implications for India: Operation Desh Vapsi and the Chabahar Exit

For India, the conflict is a direct threat to economic stability. India imports over 85% of its crude oil, with 2.7 million barrels daily transiting the Strait of Hormuz.

* **LPG Crisis**: Commercial users face 25-day waiting periods for bookings.
* **Food Security**: Interruption of ammonia imports threatens crop production in Punjab and Haryana.
* **Surat Diamond Hub**: Facing severe raw material shortages as 40% of rough stones come from the UAE and Israel.

The safety of the 1 crore (10 million) Indian nationals in the Gulf is the priority national security issue. As of March 9, 2026, the government facilitated the return of **67,000 Indians**. Strategic autonomy has also taken a hit; India has effectively liquidated its liabilities and walked away from the **Chabahar port** project to avoid US secondary sanctions.
