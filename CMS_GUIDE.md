# Median Point CMS Editor's Guide & FAQ

Welcome to the **Median Point** Content Management System (powered by Keystatic). This guide serves as an extensive FAQ and reference manual for all the custom blocks, styling options, and layout tools available in the editor.

---

## 1. Global Settings
Located under **Global Settings** in the CMS sidebar, these settings affect the entire website.

- **Global Site Theme**: Allows you to switch the site's overall color palette and style (e.g., Bloomberg, NYT, Apple, Dark Editorial). This changes the background and text colors globally.
- **Typography Preset**: Choose the default font family for the site (Modern Sans, Classic Serif, or Monospace).
- **Primary Brand Color**: Set the hex code for the primary accent color (used for links, active states, and buttons).

---

## 2. Article Meta & Frontmatter
When creating a new **Blog Post**, you must fill out the initial fields before writing the content.

- **Title**: The main headline of your article. This also generates the URL slug.
- **Save as Draft**: Check this if you are still working on the piece and don't want it published to the live site yet.
- **SEO Title & Description**: Optional fields to override the default title and description specifically for search engines and social media previews.
- **Cover Image**: The main image displayed at the top of the article and on article cards.
- **Excerpt / Short Description**: A brief summary that appears on the homepage and archive lists.
- **Category & Tags**: Used to organize your articles. The category appears above the title.
- **Heading Color**: Choose whether you want the article's headings to be the "Deep Red" brand color or standard black/default.

---

## 3. Text Formatting (Marks)
Highlight text and use the formatting toolbar to apply these styles:

- **✏️ Highlight Text**: Applies a subtle red background and an underline to emphasize a specific phrase inline.
- **Aa Small Caps**: Converts text into elegant Small Caps (often used for acronyms or formal names).
- **✨ Text Gradient**: Applies a vibrant red-to-orange gradient across the selected text.

---

## 4. Typography & Layout Wrappers
Use these blocks to change how entire paragraphs or sections behave.

- **Text Size**: Wrap a paragraph to make the text Small, Normal, Large, or Extra Large.
- **Drop Cap Paragraph**: Makes the very first letter of the paragraph massive and elegant, perfect for the opening paragraph of a long essay.
- **Custom Colored Heading**: Insert an H2, H3, or H4 that explicitly uses Brand Red, Brand Blue, or Deep Charcoal, overriding standard theme colors.
- **Typography**: The ultimate font override. Wrap any text to change its font family, weight (bold/light), letter spacing, or capitalization independently of the global theme.
- **Topline (Forbes-style Box)**: A styled box at the very top of an article containing quick bullet points or an executive summary.
- **Historical Context**: A special block that displays a prominent "Year" label (e.g., "1994" or "Cold War") to separate historical background from modern analysis.
- **Side Note**: A box that floats to the left or right of your paragraphs on desktop, keeping the main text flowing. Great for tangents or definitions.

---

## 5. Editorial Blocks
These blocks are the core of your magazine layouts.

- **Callout Box**: A colored box for notes. Choose from Info (Blue), Warning (Yellow), Breaking (Red), or Tip (Green).
- **Pull Quote**: A large, decorative quote to highlight a key statement. You can add an optional attribution/source.
- **Section Divider**: A horizontal line to break up text. Options include Ornamental (diamond motif), Plain, or Dotted.
- **Image with Caption**: A standard image block that supports captions and photo credits (e.g., "Photo: Reuters"). Can be set to full-bleed width.
- **Info Box / Fact Box**: A sidebar-style box for key facts, definitions, or data summaries.
- **Related Reading Link**: An inline card that cross-references another article on the site.

---

## 6. Advanced Layouts
Use these to build complex structures inside your article.

- **Cinematic Hero**: A massive hero section that can contain a background image, video, gradient, or solid color. Supports parallax effects and height adjustments (Full Screen vs Half Screen).
- **Content Section**: A wrapper to group other blocks together and apply a shared background color or padding.
- **Responsive Grid**: A flexible grid for placing items side-by-side. Supports 2, 3, or 4 columns with adjustable gaps.
- **Columns Grid**: Specifically for creating text columns (e.g., 50/50 split, or 1/3 and 2/3 splits).
- **Accordion (Collapsible)**: Creates a section that readers can click to expand. Great for hiding deep technical details.
- **Timeline Item**: Use multiple of these to build a vertical timeline. Requires a Date and an Event Title.
- **FAQ Section**: Specifically formatted for Q&A. Readers can toggle answers open/closed.

---

## 7. Data & Visualization
Ideal for geopolitical analysis and comparisons.

- **Comparison Table**: An analytical layout to compare features, stats, or specs between two subjects (Column A vs Column B).
- **Pros & Cons**: A split two-column list highlighting positives (green) and negatives (red).
- **Statistics Grid**: A dashboard-style grid of numbers with optional up/down trend arrows (e.g., "50% ⬆️").
- **Image Gallery**: Display multiple images in either a standard Grid or an asymmetrical Masonry layout.
- **Image Carousel**: An interactive, swipeable photo slider. Perfect for photo essays.
- **Before/After Slider**: A scrubbable image viewer. Excellent for comparing satellite imagery or maps before and after an event.
- **Google Maps Embed**: Drops an interactive map into the article. You just need the URL from the Google Maps iframe code.

---

## 8. Social Embeds & External Media
- **YouTube Video**: Embeds a video. You can use the block, or simply paste the YouTube URL on a blank line and press Enter.
- **Twitter/X Post**: Embeds a tweet. Paste the URL or the Tweet ID.
- **Instagram / Facebook**: Embed social media posts directly by pasting their URLs.
- **Custom HTML / Embed**: The ultimate escape hatch. Use this to paste raw HTML iframe codes from data visualization tools like Datawrapper, Flourish, or custom scripts.

---

## 9. Citations
- **References / Citations**: An auto-numbered citation list designed to sit at the very bottom of deep analysis pieces.
